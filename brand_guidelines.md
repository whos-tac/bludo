# Bludo Brand Guidelines

## Typography
* Primary/Headings: 'DIN Condensed Bold'
* Body Text: 'Inter' (Variable)
* Rule: Use ALL CAPS for main headings (e.g., BÜRGERMEISTER HEARING 2026).

## Color Palette
* Primary Dark Blue: #102D41 (BULME Blue — header/nav background)
* Light Blue Accent: #8cd4feff (links, highlights)
* Button Blue: #3A8FCC (distinct from department/accent blues — for interactive buttons)
* Text Main: #1A1A1A (Anthracite)
* Background Cards: #F4FFFF (Twilight Blue)
* Borders: #666666

### Department Accent Colors
* Maschinenbau: #7BB7E5
* Elektrotechnik: #DE5528
* Elektronik: #A2CB3E
* Wirtschaft: #FFE32C

## UI Behavior
* Borders: 1px solid #E2E8F0. Flat design, minimal shadows.
